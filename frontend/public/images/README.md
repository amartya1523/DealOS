# DealOS original artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for the cinematic landing redesign. Both images are conceptual brand artwork, not screenshots of the application. No reference-repository images were copied.

## Saved assets

- `frontend/public/images/momentum-sculpture.png` — hero and closing chapter, 1536×1024.
- `frontend/public/images/connected-sculpture.png` — expanding connection scene, 1536×1024.

## Final prompts

### Momentum sculpture

Create a high-end art-directed 3D abstract editorial website hero image for DealOS, a revenue operations brand. Wide landscape 1536x1024. One enormous sculptural ascending arrow made from a continuous folded inflated chrome ribbon with lime green translucent glass sections, diagonally moving from bottom left toward top right. Polished liquid silver metallic surfaces with beautiful physically realistic reflections and soft rim light. Deep almost black forest green studio background (#0b2018), with very subtle green atmospheric haze and a few small floating chrome spheres, an architectural studio floor barely visible. Object concentrated in center and right two thirds, generous dark negative space on left for HTML headline overlay. High contrast, extremely sophisticated CGI product photography, sculptural editorial, tactile brushed aluminum and optical glass, subtle film grain. No text, letters, numbers, UI, charts, dashboards, logos, or watermarks. This is a finished original website hero artwork, not a website mockup.

### Connected sculpture

Wide cinematic 3D editorial artwork for a sophisticated business software website, landscape 1536x1024. Four colossal sculptural interlocking chain links arranged diagonally from lower left to upper right, one satin silver aluminum, one pale translucent lime green glass, one dark forest green glossy ceramic, one warm ivory stone. Floating in a minimal pale warm sage studio with physically accurate soft shadows, light beams and elegant caustics. A visual metaphor for connected business stages, minimalist architecture meets luxury product photography. Highly tactile photoreal materials, dramatic depth of field, art directed asymmetrical composition, beautiful negative space at left. Entire objects visible with breathing room. No text, lettering, logos, charts, dashboard, UI or watermark.
